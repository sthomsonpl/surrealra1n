Apply dropbear-rootless-shells.patch with -p1, then configure for
arm-apple-darwin with an iPhoneOS deployment target of 15.0. The Procursus
compatibility mappings used by the bundled binary are:

  getpwnam=ie_getpwnam
  getpwuid=ie_getpwuid
  getgrnam=ie_getgrnam
  execv=ie_execv

Link against libiosexec.1.dylib and libcrypt.2.dylib, set an LC_RPATH of
/var/jb/usr/lib, enable dead stripping, and build PROGRAMS=dropbear. The final
binary carries the same platform-application, no-container, and
skip-library-validation entitlements as the Procursus package binary.
